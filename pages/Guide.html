How to Use IDN E-Report

Follow these simple steps to submit your report through IDN E-Report. Choose the appropriate category, provide clear information, and submit your concern to the responsible team.

Start by selecting the type of report that best describes your concern.

  1. Academic — Report issues related to learning, teachers, schedules, assignments, or other academic matters.
  2. Facility — Report problems related to classrooms, equipment, buildings, or other school facilities.
  3. Anonymous — Submit a report while keeping your identity private.

Explain your concern clearly and provide enough information for the report to be understood.

Include information such as:
  1. What happened?
  2. Where did it happen?
  3. When did it happen?
  4. What problem needs attention?
  5. Any additional information that may help.
Tip: Keep your report clear, factual, and respectful.

If necessary, provide additional information that can help the team understand the situation.

This may include:
  • Photos
  • Screenshots
  • Relevant documents
  • Other supporting details
Note: Only provide information that is relevant to your report.

Before submitting, check your report carefully.

Make sure:
  1. The correct category is selected.
  2. The description is clear.
  3. The information provided is accurate.
  4. Any attached files are relevant.
A clear report helps the responsible team understand the issue more easily.

Once everything is ready, click Submit Report. Your report will be sent to the authorized team for review and further action.

Submit responsibly and make sure your report contains accurate information.

After submitting your report, you can check its progress through the report status.

Possible statuses:
Pending → In Review → In Progress → Resolved
  • Pending — Your report has been received and is waiting for review.
  • In Review — The report is currently being reviewed.
  • In Progress — The issue is being handled or followed up.
  • Resolved — The reported issue has been addressed.

Need to Report Something?

Your voice matters. If you encounter an academic issue, facility problem, or other concern, use IDN E-Report to share it with the appropriate team.
